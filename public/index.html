    Scan my linksReads your GitHub repositories, your site and the links above for skills and projects, on this computer. Nothing is added until you say so.

    Best match learns from you

    Roles you save, apply to or contact count for; roles you hide count against. Best match then leans toward roles like the ones you go for. It is worked out in this window from your own pipeline, and nothing is sent anywhere.

    Phone alerts

    Free, via the ntfy app
    1. Install ntfy and subscribe to a topic like
    2. Enter it here and send a test.
    3. Turn on the bell for a saved view.

    Public ntfy topics can be read by anyone who guesses the name, so pick a long random one.

    LinkedIn searches

    Public listings, no account

    One search per line: keywords | location. Add | remote for remote-only. Up to 12; fewer is faster. Leave empty to turn LinkedIn off. Checked every 4 minutes; you get a push only for roles posted in the last 2 hours.

    Browser extension

    Open any job page in Edge or Chrome, click the Lookout button, and see which of the role's skills you have, whether it asks for more experience than you have, and whether Lookout already found it. On your GitHub, Hugging Face, Stack Overflow, Kaggle or LeetCode profile the button shows a dot: click it to add the skills that page shows. It reads a page only when you click, and sends it only to this Lookout.

    1. In Edge or Chrome open edge://extensions or chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
    2. Choose Load unpacked and pick the folder
    3. Open the extension's options and paste this pairing key.

    Profiles

    Each has its own résumé, skills, saved views and pipeline.

    New profile

    Paste your résumé

    Copy everything from your résumé and paste it here. It stays on this computer.

    Start with your résumé

    Give Lookout your résumé and it fills in your skills, education and experience, then ranks every role against them. It is read on this computer and never uploaded.

    Share your list

    Saves a file anyone can import into their own Lookout. It holds careers boards and job lists only: nothing about you, your profile or your pipeline.

    Remove all boards and lists?

    Lookout stops checking . Roles already found stay until they age out, and companies you watch by hand are kept. Built-in community lists are switched off; you can switch any back on in Sources.
